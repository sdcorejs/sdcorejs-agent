# Short video

Return scenes or beats with spoken line, visual action and on-screen text.
Make the opening specific and pay it off. Use concrete examples and natural
spoken rhythm; read aloud to estimate timing and trim competing messages.
Offer practical footage ideas without inventing ownership or firsthand events.
Captions support comprehension. Product demonstration claims must reflect real
capability; mark an unverified demo as a proposed shot, never captured proof.
