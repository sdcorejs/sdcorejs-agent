# Text post

Use a clear opening, useful body and optional relevant CTA. A short caption
may be one or two sentences. A longer post needs a readable progression and
paragraphs. Match the supplied audience/voice; remove generic inspirational
filler. Preserve claim conditions, avoid fake first-person experience, and keep
source notes outside the public copy where possible.
