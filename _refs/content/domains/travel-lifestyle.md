# Travel and lifestyle

Separate the author's real experience from research. If they have not visited,
write "an idea for a weekend walk" or another research-grounded perspective,
not "I loved the sunset here". Supplied experience may use first person but must
not gain invented details. Date prices, seasons, access and opening information;
verify current logistics if a reader might rely on them. Avoid guarantees of
safety, accessibility or availability without evidence. Practical context can
include trip style, distance, cost source/date and known limitations.
