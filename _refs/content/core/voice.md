# Separate personal and product voice

Keep separate identity IDs, evidence samples and accepted rules. Extract concrete
patterns from real writing: opening, sentence rhythm, level of detail, pronouns,
humor, vocabulary, closing/CTA and phrases to avoid. Show a short tentative
sample if evidence is thin; do not claim a learned voice from three adjectives.

Personal voice may express opinion and supplied experience. Product voice
speaks for the brand and uses verified facts and approved positioning. The same
feature fact can appear in both, but never give the personal author an invented
customer result or let a brand claim the author's private history.

Store accepted rules separately from proposed observations. Apply requested
edits in-session; persistent changes require exact profile owner/path authority.
A profile records samples/source dates, accepted rule, rationale, exclusions and
revision. Never silently merge personal and product profiles or agent persona.
