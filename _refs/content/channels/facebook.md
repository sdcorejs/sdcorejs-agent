# Facebook

Identify profile, page or group and its audience when that changes the draft.
Lead with a specific useful idea, use readable paragraphs, and give enough
context for a reader encountering the post alone. A personal post may use a
real anecdote; a product page may lead with a supported customer problem and
benefit. A group post should respect known group norms and disclosure needs.

Use an invitation to discuss only when it fits the purpose; avoid manufactured
engagement bait. Provide image/alt-text ideas only when useful and distinguish
ideas from created assets. Links, preview rendering and placement constraints
should be checked in the actual destination if material. No universal hashtag,
frequency, reach or algorithm claims. Paid campaign requirements need current
platform/account evidence and explicit scope.
