# TikTok

Design an opening moment, a clear progression and a payoff that matches the
promise. Write spoken language that sounds natural aloud. Pair narration with
visual action/on-screen text only when it adds clarity; do not make viewers
read dense prose while listening to different facts.

Label scenes, voiceover/dialogue, on-screen text and practical B-roll ideas.
Use approximate timing as a production estimate, then check by reading aloud.
Include captions/accessibility and a simple CTA when useful. Distinguish sourced
visual suggestions from footage actually owned/created. Check current account
features, format limits and music rights when needed for production. Do not
claim trending audio or a hook guarantees distribution.
