# Repurpose

Extract the source's central point, supported claims and reusable examples.
Choose an angle for each destination and preserve relevant qualifiers. Facebook
may use a self-contained story or useful list; TikTok needs spoken beats, visual
progression and payoff. A carousel needs slide logic, not paragraph slices.

State which identity speaks in each version. Retain product truth while adapting
personal versus brand voice. Do not convert researched source into a personal
experience, a quote into an endorsement, or an opinion into a benchmark.
Return only requested variants with minimal adaptation/source notes.
