# Editorial review

Read the draft as its intended reader. Is the useful point clear? Does the
opening deliver what the body promises? Is there one coherent purpose, natural
voice, concrete detail and a proportionate CTA? Remove repetition, empty hype
and ambiguity without erasing the author's stance.

Check every material factual/experience/customer/platform claim against supplied
or researched evidence. Unsupported numbers are not made safe by adding a
confident tone. Check personal/product narrator separation, sensitive details,
format flow, accessibility and whether the result can stand alone.

Review-only: return precise editorial observations and suggested revisions;
do not modify files or claim code has been tested. Edit intent: return improved
copy and only material notes. Scorecards are optional; a single opaque AI score
cannot replace factual and editorial judgment or author acceptance.
