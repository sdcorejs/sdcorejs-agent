# Research and claims

Maintain a compact claim ledger only when it helps: claim, type, source,
verification date, scope/conditions, supported wording and unresolved gap.
Types: verified fact, user-supplied fact, personal experience, opinion, inference,
marketing comparison, customer quote, platform limit and account observation.
User-supplied facts are attributed as supplied, not independently verified.

For external facts prefer primary/current sources. A URL is not proof until its
relevant passage is read. Benchmark comparisons need versions, workload,
baseline, environment and method. Testimonials need the actual quote, speaker
permission for the intended use and no invented endorsement. First-person
experience requires the author's supplied experience, not a travel source.

Retain a supported claim with its conditions. Rewrite/remove unsupported claims
from the usable draft; separately say what evidence would enable the stronger
claim. Opinion can remain opinion without false numbers. Do not stall unrelated
copy while waiting for optional proof. Do not invent citations or fill a metric
placeholder with a plausible value.

Platform guidance distinguishes stable editorial advice, verified hard limits,
heuristics and account observations. Recheck limits before use if stale or no
match exists. Never promise an algorithm boost, fixed hashtag count or universal
best posting time from anecdote or outdated platform advice.
