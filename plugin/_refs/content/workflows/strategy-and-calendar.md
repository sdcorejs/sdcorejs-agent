# Strategy and editorial calendar

Define audience, purpose, positioning, content themes and evidence available.
Turn themes into useful angles and concrete deliverables with identity, channel,
format, source needs, production effort and review owner. Propose a cadence from
actual resources and goals; no universal posting-frequency promise.

A calendar is a draft artifact: date/time assumptions, timezone, status,
owner and source/review dependencies. It grants no scheduling or publication
permission. Keep personal and product series distinct while reusing verified
facts. Set a review window and real success signals; use learning-from-results
for feedback rather than claiming causality from a small sample.
