# Tech

Record versions and environment when they matter. Verify code behavior if a
claim depends on it, or label the example illustrative/unexecuted. Benchmark
claims require workload, baseline and method; "10x faster" without data becomes
a specific supported capability or an explicitly unverified hypothesis outside
public copy. Explain who benefits, when the advice applies and its tradeoffs.
One project experience is not universal proof. Preserve product facts across
personal and brand voice; do not invent a deployment history.
