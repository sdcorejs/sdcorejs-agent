# Marketing and business

Connect audience problem to a supported benefit, proof point, offer and
proportionate CTA. Distinguish feature from outcome; no invented ROI or urgency.
Keep testimonials faithful, attributed and authorized for the intended use.
A compliment is not a controlled benchmark or proof of typical results.
Separate organic editorial content from paid advertising requirements. Use
actual positioning and commercial terms; surface material missing offer facts
without blocking a neutral draft. Never manufacture scarcity or customer logos.
