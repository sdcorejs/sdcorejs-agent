# Long form

State the reader's question and answer it with a coherent argument, concrete
examples, evidence and limitations. Use headings to organize meaning; avoid
padding to reach a word count. Distinguish experience, research and opinion.
Keep sources traceable and preserve conditions when summarizing comparisons.
A long-form article can be an editorial source for multiple shorter outputs;
select one useful angle for each rather than compressing every paragraph.
